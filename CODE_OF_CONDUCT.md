# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, as its code of conduct.

In short: participate respectfully and in good faith. Be constructive, assume
the best of others, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment and
exclusionary behavior are not welcome here.

## Reporting

Concerns or possible violations can be reported privately to the maintainer at
**david.bickford@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially and reviewed in
good faith.
